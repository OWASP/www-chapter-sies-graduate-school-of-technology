## Chapter Inauguration Ceremony

The OWASP SIES Graduate School of Technology Student Chapter was officially inaugurated on **23 January 2026**, marking the beginning of a new initiative focused on cybersecurity awareness, learning, and hands-on skill development within the institute.

The inauguration ceremony was graced by the presence of the **Principal of SIES Graduate School of Technology**, along with the **Chairperson - Vedant Borade**, **Head of Department - Sulochana Madachane**, and respected members of the faculty. Their presence and encouragement highlighted the importance of cybersecurity education and industry-relevant learning for students.

During the event, the **Chairperson** addressed the gathering and shared insights on the vision and objectives of the OWASP student chapter. The speech emphasized creating a strong technical community, promoting ethical security practices, and providing students with opportunities to explore real-world cybersecurity challenges.

Faculty members expressed their support for the initiative and encouraged students to actively participate in upcoming workshops, Capture The Flag competitions, and technical sessions organized under the chapter.

The inauguration concluded with an interactive session where students learned about OWASP, the goals of the SIES GST student chapter, and the roadmap for future events. This ceremony marked the foundation of a collaborative and security-focused student community at SIES GST.
